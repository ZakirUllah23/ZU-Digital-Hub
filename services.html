Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services, process, pricing, and how we work.

What digital marketing and web services does ZU Digital Hub offer?

ZU Digital Hub is a full-service agency providing Social Media Advertising (Meta & TikTok Ads), Google Ads PPC campaigns, custom Website Design & Development, Professional Video Editing, AI-Generated Video Creation, Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Social Media Management, and Graphic Design.

Where is ZU Digital Hub located, and do you work with international clients?

We are headquartered at Al Jameel Plaza in Nowshera, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan. We serve businesses locally across Pakistan as well as international brands in the United States, United Kingdom, UAE, and worldwide via seamless remote collaboration.

How long does a website development or marketing campaign launch take?

Landing pages and single video edits are typically delivered in 2–4 business days. Multi-page business websites take 5–7 days, while full e-commerce platforms take 10–14 days. Paid ad campaigns are researched, created, and launched within 48 to 72 hours of project onboarding.

Are your packages and pricing transparent?

Yes, absolutely. We publish transparent rates in both USD and PKR for all standard tiers (Starter, Standard, and Premium) directly on our Packages & Pricing page with zero hidden charges.

How do you track and report campaign results?

We establish clear tracking through Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, and conversion tracking. You receive transparent performance reports detailing impressions, click-through rates (CTR), cost per acquisition (CPA), conversion rates, and revenue ROI.
