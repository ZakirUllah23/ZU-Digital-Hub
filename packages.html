Understanding Our Advertising Pricing

Our advertising packages show three figures: Service Fee (paid to ZU Digital Hub for managing your campaign), Ad Spend (the budget that goes directly to the platform — e.g. Facebook, Google), and the Agency-Managed Total. If you pay the platform directly, you only pay us the Service Fee. If you prefer ZU Digital Hub to handle all payments, you pay the Agency-Managed Total which includes both the service fee and ad spend.
